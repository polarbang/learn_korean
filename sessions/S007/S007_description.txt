Comprehensible Korean Input for Beginners | A day at a Korean bakery café

Come explore a bakery café in Korea with me! We find a parking spot, look around the different floors, choose bread and coffee, and visit the rooftop garden.

Follow the actions and learn everyday Korean words for places, food, and things you do at a café. This video is for beginners who want to practice listening to Korean in a real-life situation.
