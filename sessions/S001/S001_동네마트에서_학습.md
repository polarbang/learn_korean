## How to read this page
- **Sound** = how the word sounds, written in English letters.
- Korean verbs and adjectives change their ending, but they are **still the same word**.
  The **bold part** stays the same — only the ending changes.

### 사다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **사**다 | sa-da |
| Present | **사**요 | sa-yo |
| Past | **샀**어요 | sa-sseo-yo |
| Future | **살** 거예요 | sal kkeo-ye-yo |

**Examples**
1. 우유도 **사요**.
   *u-yu-do sa-yo.*
   → I'll buy milk too.
2. 친구 생일 선물을 **샀어요**.
   *chin-gu saeng-il seon-mu-reul sa-sseo-yo.*
   → I bought a birthday present for my friend.
3. 주말에 옷을 **살 거예요**.
   *ju-ma-re o-seul sal kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → I'm going to buy clothes on the weekend.

### 고르다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **고르**다 | go-reu-da |
| Present | **골라**요 | gol-la-yo |
| Past | **골랐**어요 | gol-la-sseo-yo |
| Future | **고를** 거예요 | go-reul kkeo-ye-yo |

⚠ **Irregular:** 고르 + 아요 becomes **골라요** (an extra ㄹ appears). It's still the same word.

**Examples**
1. 사과를 **골라요**. 이거? 아니면 이거?
   *sa-gwa-reul gol-la-yo. i-geo? a-ni-myeon i-geo?*
   → I'm choosing apples. This one? Or this one?
2. 카페에서 커피를 **골랐어요**.
   *ka-pe-e-seo keo-pi-reul gol-la-sseo-yo.*
   → I chose a coffee at the café.
3. 친구 선물은 제가 **고를 거예요**.
   *chin-gu seon-mu-reun je-ga go-reul kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → I'll choose my friend's present.

### 비싸다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **비싸**다 | bi-ssa-da |
| Present | **비싸**요 | bi-ssa-yo |
| Past | **비쌌**어요 | bi-ssa-sseo-yo |

**Examples**
1. 와, 소고기는 정말 **비싸요**!
   *wa, so-go-gi-neun jeong-mal bi-ssa-yo!*

### 싸다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **싸**다 | ssa-da |
| Present | **싸**요 | ssa-yo |
| Past | **쌌**어요 | ssa-sseo-yo |

**Examples**

   → This T-shirt was really cheap.

### 조금

**Examples**

## 문법 (Grammar): -보다 (than)

Use **보다** to compare two things. Put 보다 right after the thing you compare *against*.

> **A은/는 B보다 [adjective]** = A is more [adjective] than B

**은 or 는?** If A ends in a vowel, use **는** (소고기**는**). If A ends in a consonant, use **은** (과일**은**).

| Pork | than beef | is cheap |
|---|---|---|
| 돼지고기는 | 소고기**보다** | 싸요 |
| *dwae-ji-go-gi-neun* | *so-go-gi-bo-da* | *ssa-yo* |

→ Pork is cheaper than beef.

The adjective itself doesn't change — 싸요 already means "cheaper" when 보다 is in the sentence.

**Examples**
1. 소고기**는** 돼지고기**보다** 비싸요.
   *so-go-gi-neun dwae-ji-go-gi-bo-da bi-ssa-yo.*
   → Beef is more expensive than pork.
2. 과일**은** 고기**보다** 싸요.
   *gwa-i-reun go-gi-bo-da ssa-yo.*
   → Fruit is cheaper than meat.
3. 시장**은** 마트**보다** 작아요.
   *si-jang-eun ma-teu-bo-da ja-ga-yo.*
   → The market is smaller than the supermarket.
