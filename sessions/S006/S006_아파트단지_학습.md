## How to read this page

- **Sound** = how the word sounds, written in English letters.
- Korean verbs and adjectives change their endings, but they are **still the same word**. The **bold part** shows the word as it changes.
- **From the video** quotes the narration. **Try it elsewhere** gives a new situation to practice.

## 동사 (Verbs)

| Korean | Meaning | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 입다 | to put on or wear clothes | ip-tta |
| 타다 | to get on, to ride | ta-da |
| 누르다 | to press | nu-reu-da |

### 입다

| Form | Korean | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dictionary form | **입**다 | ip-tta |
| Present | **입**어요 | i-beo-yo |
| Past | **입**었어요 | i-beo-sseo-yo |
| Future | **입**을 거예요 | i-beul kkeo-ye-yo |

**From the video**

1. 패딩을 **입어요**.  
   *pae-ding-eul i-beo-yo.*  
   → I put on a padded jacket.

**Try it elsewhere**

2. 아침에 코트를 **입어요**.  
   *a-chi-me ko-teu-reul i-beo-yo.*  
   → I put on a coat in the morning.

### 타다

| Form | Korean | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dictionary form | **타**다 | ta-da |
| Present | **타**요 | ta-yo |
| Past | **탔**어요 | ta-sseo-yo |
| Future | **탈** 거예요 | tal kkeo-ye-yo |

**From the video**

1. 엘리베이터에 **타요**.  
   *el-li-be-i-teo-e ta-yo.*  
   → I get into the elevator.

**Try it elsewhere**

2. 내일 버스를 **탈 거예요**.  
   *nae-il beo-seu-reul tal kkeo-ye-yo.*  
   → I'll take the bus tomorrow.

With an elevator, the video says **엘리베이터에 타요**. With a bus, **버스를 타요** is common.

### 누르다

| Form | Korean | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dictionary form | **누르**다 | nu-reu-da |
| Present | **눌러**요 | nul-leo-yo |
| Past | **눌렀**어요 | nul-leo-sseo-yo |
| Future | **누를** 거예요 | nu-reul kkeo-ye-yo |

⚠ **누르다 → 눌러요**: the 르 changes to ㄹㄹ before 어요.

**From the video**

1. 버튼을 **눌러요**.  
   *beo-teu-neul nul-leo-yo.*  
   → I press the button.

**Try it elsewhere**

2. 전화기 버튼을 **눌러요**.  
   *jeon-hwa-gi beo-teu-neul nul-leo-yo.*  
   → I press a button on the phone.

## 형용사 (Adjectives)

### 춥다

| Form | Korean | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dictionary form | **춥**다 | chup-tta |
| Present | **추워**요 | chu-wo-yo |
| Past | **추웠**어요 | chu-wo-sseo-yo |

⚠ **춥다 → 추워요**: the ㅂ changes to 우 before 어요.

**From the video**

1. 날씨가 진짜 **추웠어요**.  
   *nal-ssi-ga jin-jja chu-wo-sseo-yo.*  
   → It was really cold outside.

**Try it elsewhere**

2. 오늘 아침은 **추워요**.  
   *o-neul a-chi-meun chu-wo-yo.*  
   → It's cold this morning.

## 문법 (Grammar): N에는 N이/가 있어요 (there is/are)

Use **-에는** after a place and **-이/가 있어요** after the thing found there. Use **이** after a final consonant and **가** after a vowel.

> **[place]에는 [thing]이/가 있어요** = There is/are [thing] at/in [place].

| Place | Thing | What is there? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 지하에는 | 헬스장이 | 있어요 |
| *ji-ha-e-neun* | *hel-sseu-jang-i* | *i-sseo-yo* |

→ There is a gym in the basement.

**From the video**

1. 단지 안에는 **편의점이 있어요**.  
   *dan-ji a-ne-neun pyeo-ni-jeo-mi i-sseo-yo.*  
   → There is a convenience store inside the complex.

2. 지하에는 **헬스장이 있어요**.  
   *ji-ha-e-neun hel-sseu-jang-i i-sseo-yo.*  
   → There is a gym in the basement.

**Try it elsewhere**

3. 우리 동네에는 **카페가 있어요**.  
   *u-ri dong-ne-e-neun ka-pe-ga i-sseo-yo.*  
   → There is a café in my neighborhood.
