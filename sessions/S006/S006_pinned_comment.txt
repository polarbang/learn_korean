🔐 In the video, 비밀번호 (bi-mil-beon-ho) means “password.” In everyday conversation, we often shorten it to 비번 (bi-beon).

Here are two more Korean short forms:
아이스 아메리카노 → 아아 (iced Americano) ☕
카카오톡 → 카톡 (KakaoTalk) 💬
