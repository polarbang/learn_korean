## How to read this page
- **Sound** = how the word sounds, written in English letters.
- Korean verbs and adjectives change their ending, but they are **still the same word**.
  The **bold part** stays the same — only the ending changes.

### 닦다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **닦**다 | dak-tta |
| Present | **닦**아요 | da-kka-yo |
| Past | **닦**았어요 | da-kka-sseo-yo |
| Future | **닦**을 거예요 | da-kkeul kkeo-ye-yo |

닦다 means *to wipe* (with a towel or cloth). For teeth, Korean also uses 닦다: **이를 닦다** = to brush your teeth.

**Examples**
1. 칫솔로 이를 **닦아요**.
   *chit-ssol-lo i-reul da-kka-yo.*
   → I brush my teeth with a toothbrush.
2. 수건으로 얼굴을 **닦았어요**.
   *su-geo-neu-ro eol-gu-reul da-kka-sseo-yo.*
   → I dried my face with a towel.
3. 밥을 먹고 이를 **닦을 거예요**.
   *ba-beul meok-kko i-reul da-kkeul kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → I'll brush my teeth after I eat.

### 쓰다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **쓰**다 | sseu-da |
| Present | **써**요 | sseo-yo |
| Past | **썼**어요 | sseo-sseo-yo |
| Future | **쓸** 거예요 | sseul kkeo-ye-yo |

⚠ **Irregular:** 쓰 + 어요 becomes **써요** (the ㅡ drops out). It's still the same word.

쓰다 also means *to write*: 편지를 써요 = I write a letter. The forms are the same.

**Examples**
1. 저는 비누를 **써요**. 핸드워시도 **써요**.
   *jeo-neun bi-nu-reul sseo-yo. haen-deu-wo-si-do sseo-yo.*
   → I use bar soap. I use hand wash, too.
2. 어제 친구 컵을 **썼어요**.
   *eo-je chin-gu keo-beul sseo-sseo-yo.*
   → I used my friend's cup yesterday.
3. 내일 새 수건을 **쓸 거예요**.
   *nae-il sae su-geo-neul sseul kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → I'll use a new towel tomorrow.

### 틀다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **틀**다 | teul-da |
| Present | **틀**어요 | teu-reo-yo |
| Past | **틀**었어요 | teu-reo-sseo-yo |
| Future | **틀** 거예요 | teul kkeo-ye-yo |

⚠ **Irregular:** in the future form, 틀 already ends in ㄹ, so it's just **틀 거예요** (not 틀을). It's still the same word.

틀다 is for things that *flow* or *play*: water (물을 틀다), music (음악을 틀다), the air conditioner (에어컨을 틀다).

**Examples**
1. 물을 **틀어요**.
   *mu-reul teu-reo-yo.*
   → I turn on the water.
2. 더워요. 그래서 에어컨을 **틀었어요**.
   *deo-wo-yo. geu-rae-seo e-eo-keo-neul teu-reo-sseo-yo.*
   → It's hot. So I turned on the air conditioner.
3. 파티에서 음악을 **틀 거예요**.
   *pa-ti-e-seo eu-ma-geul teul kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → I'll play music at the party.

### 세수하다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **세수하**다 | se-su-ha-da |
| Present | **세수해**요 | se-su-hae-yo |
| Past | **세수했**어요 | se-su-hae-sseo-yo |
| Future | **세수할** 거예요 | se-su-hal kkeo-ye-yo |

In the video you hear **세수를 해요** — the same meaning. 세수 is only for your face; for hands, say 손을 씻다.

**Examples**
1. 아침에 **세수해요**.
   *a-chi-me se-su-hae-yo.*
   → I wash my face in the morning.
2. 찬물로 **세수했어요**.
   *chan-mul-lo se-su-hae-sseo-yo.*
   → I washed my face with cold water.
3. 지금 **세수할 거예요**.
   *ji-geum se-su-hal kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → I'm going to wash my face now.

### 묻히다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **묻히**다 | mu-chi-da |
| Present | **묻혀**요 | mu-chyeo-yo |
| Past | **묻혔**어요 | mu-chyeo-sseo-yo |
| Future | **묻힐** 거예요 | mu-chil kkeo-ye-yo |

묻히다 = to put something (foam, soap, sauce) on a surface — or to get it on something by accident.

**Examples**
1. 얼굴에 거품을 **묻혀요**.
   *eol-gu-re geo-pu-meul mu-chyeo-yo.*
   → I put foam on my face.
2. 옷에 커피를 **묻혔어요**.
   *o-se keo-pi-reul mu-chyeo-sseo-yo.*
   → I got coffee on my clothes.
3. 손에 비누를 **묻힐 거예요**.
   *so-ne bi-nu-reul mu-chil kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → I'll put soap on my hands.

### 씻다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **씻**다 | ssit-tta |
| Present | **씻**어요 | ssi-seo-yo |
| Past | **씻**었어요 | ssi-seo-sseo-yo |
| Future | **씻**을 거예요 | ssi-seul kkeo-ye-yo |

씻다 on its own means *to wash up* (take a shower).

**Examples**
1. 칫솔을 물로 **씻어요**.
   *chit-sso-reul mul-lo ssi-seo-yo.*
   → I wash the toothbrush with water.
2. 사과를 **씻었어요**. 그리고 먹었어요.
   *sa-gwa-reul ssi-seo-sseo-yo. geu-ri-go meo-geo-sseo-yo.*
   → I washed the apple. And I ate it.
3. 운동했어요. 그래서 지금 **씻을 거예요**.
   *un-dong-hae-sseo-yo. geu-rae-seo ji-geum ssi-seul kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → I worked out. So I'm going to wash up now.

### 바꾸다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **바꾸**다 | ba-kku-da |
| Present | **바꿔**요 | ba-kkwo-yo |
| Past | **바꿨**어요 | ba-kkwo-sseo-yo |
| Future | **바꿀** 거예요 | ba-kkul kkeo-ye-yo |

**Examples**
1. 수건을 새 수건으로 **바꿔요**.
   *su-geo-neul sae su-geo-neu-ro ba-kkwo-yo.*
   → I change the towel for a new one.
2. 어제 휴대폰을 **바꿨어요**.
   *eo-je hyu-dae-po-neul ba-kkwo-sseo-yo.*
   → I got a new phone yesterday.
3. 다음 주에 머리 색을 **바꿀 거예요**.
   *da-eum ju-e meo-ri sae-geul ba-kkul kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → I'm going to change my hair color next week.

### 걸다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **걸**다 | geol-da |
| Present | **걸**어요 | geo-reo-yo |
| Past | **걸**었어요 | geo-reo-sseo-yo |
| Future | **걸** 거예요 | geol kkeo-ye-yo |

⚠ **Irregular:** in the future form, 걸 already ends in ㄹ, so it's just **걸 거예요** (not 걸을). It's still the same word.

Careful: **걸어요** can also mean *I walk* (from 걷다). The sentence tells you which one: 수건을 걸어요 = I hang the towel.

**Examples**
1. 수건을 **걸어요**.
   *su-geo-neul geo-reo-yo.*
   → I hang up the towel.
2. 벽에 사진을 **걸었어요**.
   *byeo-ge sa-ji-neul geo-reo-sseo-yo.*
   → I hung a photo on the wall.
3. 이따가 엄마한테 전화를 **걸 거예요**.
   *i-tta-ga eom-ma-han-te jeon-hwa-reul geol kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → I'll call my mom later. (전화를 걸다 = to make a phone call)

### 마르다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **마르**다 | ma-reu-da |
| Present | **말라**요 | mal-la-yo |
| Past | **말랐**어요 | mal-la-sseo-yo |
| Future | **마를** 거예요 | ma-reul kkeo-ye-yo |

⚠ **Irregular:** 마르 + 아요 becomes **말라요** (an extra ㄹ appears). It's still the same word.

**Examples**
1. 칫솔을 걸어요. 칫솔이 **말라요**.
   *chit-sso-reul geo-reo-yo. chit-sso-ri mal-la-yo.*
   → I hang up the toothbrush. It dries.
2. 수건이 **말랐어요**.
   *su-geo-ni mal-la-sseo-yo.*
   → The towel has dried.
3. 오늘 날씨가 좋아요. 그래서 빨래가 빨리 **마를 거예요**.
   *o-neul nal-ssi-ga jo-a-yo. geu-rae-seo ppal-lae-ga ppal-li ma-reul kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → The weather is nice today. So the laundry will dry fast.

### 누르다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **누르**다 | nu-reu-da |
| Present | **눌러**요 | nul-leo-yo |
| Past | **눌렀**어요 | nul-leo-sseo-yo |
| Future | **누를** 거예요 | nu-reul kkeo-ye-yo |

⚠ **Irregular:** 누르 + 어요 becomes **눌러요** (an extra ㄹ appears). It's still the same word.

**Examples**
1. 샴푸 펌프를 **눌러요**.
   *syam-pu peom-peu-reul nul-leo-yo.*
   → I press the shampoo pump.
2. 엘리베이터 버튼을 **눌렀어요**.
   *el-li-be-i-teo beo-teu-neul nul-leo-sseo-yo.*
   → I pressed the elevator button.
3. 이제 이 버튼을 **누를 거예요**.
   *i-je i beo-teu-neul nu-reul kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → Now I'm going to press this button.

### 따뜻하다

| | Korean | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Dictionary form | **따뜻하**다 | tta-tteu-ta-da |
| Present | **따뜻해**요 | tta-tteu-tae-yo |
| Past | **따뜻했**어요 | tta-tteu-tae-sseo-yo |

In the video you hear **따뜻한 물** = warm water (before a noun, 따뜻하다 becomes 따뜻한).

**Examples**
1. 물이 **따뜻해요**.
   *mu-ri tta-tteu-tae-yo.*
   → The water is warm.
2. 오늘은 날씨가 **따뜻해요**.
   *o-neu-reun nal-ssi-ga tta-tteu-tae-yo.*
   → The weather is warm today.
3. 어제 카페가 정말 **따뜻했어요**.
   *eo-je ka-pe-ga jeong-mal tta-tteu-tae-sseo-yo.*
   → The café was really warm yesterday.

## 부사 (Adverbs)

| Korean | Meaning | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 골고루 | evenly, all over | gol-go-ru |
| 다시 | again | da-si |

### 골고루

**Examples**
1. 얼굴을 **골고루** 씻어요.
   *eol-gu-reul gol-go-ru ssi-seo-yo.*
   → I wash my whole face, all over.
2. 채소도 고기도 **골고루** 먹어요.
   *chae-so-do go-gi-do gol-go-ru meo-geo-yo.*
   → I eat a bit of everything — vegetables and meat.

### 다시

**Examples**
1. **다시** 손을 씻어요.
   *da-si so-neul ssi-seo-yo.*
   → I wash my hands again.
2. 내일 **다시** 올 거예요.
   *nae-il da-si ol kkeo-ye-yo.*
   → I'll come again tomorrow.

## 문법 (Grammar): -(으)면 (if, when)

Put **-(으)면** on a verb to say *when this happens, that happens* — perfect for explaining how things work.
After a final consonant (받침) use **-으면** (씻**으면**), after a vowel or ㄹ use **-면** (누르**면**, 돌리**면**).

> **[verb]-(으)면 [result]** = If / When [verb], [result].

| The pump | if you press | body wash | comes out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 펌프를 | 누르**면** | 바디워시가 | 나와요 |
| *peom-peu-reul* | *nu-reu-myeon* | *ba-di-wo-si-ga* | *na-wa-yo* |

→ If you press the pump, body wash comes out.

**Examples**
1. 왼쪽으로 돌리**면** 따뜻한 물이 나와요.
   *oen-jjo-geu-ro dol-li-myeon tta-tteu-tan mu-ri na-wa-yo.*
   → If you turn it to the left, warm water comes out.
2. 칫솔을 걸어 두**면** 칫솔이 말라요.
   *chit-sso-reul geo-reo du-myeon chit-sso-ri mal-la-yo.*
   → If you hang the toothbrush up, it dries.
3. 손을 씻**으면** 기분이 좋아요.
   *so-neul ssi-seu-myeon gi-bu-ni jo-a-yo.*
   → When I wash my hands, I feel good.
